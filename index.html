Calculation method

  1. Linearly interpolate the radial profile.
  2. Add every sprinkler contribution reaching each point.
  3. Sort depths for low-quarter DU.
  4. Compute Christiansen CU from absolute deviations.

About the radial functions

Readings are 60-minute, zero-wind catch depths at 2-foot radial increments. Between readings, the calculator uses exact piecewise-linear interpolation; beyond the tested radius the contribution is zero. This avoids overshoot and negative depths from high-order polynomial fits.

Instructional limitation: Field performance also depends on wind, pressure variation, riser geometry, rotation speed, wear, and maintenance.
