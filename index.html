Calculation method

  1. Linearly interpolate the radial profile.
  2. Add every sprinkler contribution reaching each point.
  3. Normalize each point by the scenario mean for the map.
  4. Sort actual depths for low-quarter DU.
  5. Compute Christiansen CU from absolute deviations.

About the radial functions

Readings are zero-wind catch depths at the radial increment recorded for each test. Between readings, the calculator uses exact piecewise-linear interpolation; beyond the tested radius the contribution is zero. This avoids overshoot and negative depths from high-order polynomial fits.

Instructional limitation: Field performance also depends on wind, pressure variation, riser geometry, rotation speed, wear, and maintenance.
